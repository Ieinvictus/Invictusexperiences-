Privacy & Policy Invictus Experiences

Invictus Experiences respects your privacy. We securely collect basic information for bookings and never share your data without consent.

Introduction

At Invictus Experiences, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled securely and responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you interact with our website or book our trekking and camping services.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information including your name, email address, phone number, billing details, and payment information when you book a tour, subscribe to newsletters, or contact us. We only collect information that is necessary for providing our services.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process bookings, confirm reservations, communicate trip details, provide customer support, and improve our services. With your consent, we may also send promotional offers and updates about upcoming adventures.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or alteration. Payment transactions are processed through secure gateways.

Cookies Policy

Our website may use cookies to enhance user experience and analyze website traffic. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. However, we may share necessary information with trusted service providers such as hotels, transportation partners, and payment processors strictly for service fulfillment.

User Consent

By using our website or booking a tour with Invictus Experiences, you consent to the collection and use of your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as required to fulfill booking obligations or comply with legal requirements under Indian law.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to your personal data, correct inaccuracies, request deletion, or withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal limitations.

Marketing Communications

With your permission, we may send promotional emails regarding trekking packages, seasonal offers, and travel updates. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link provided in our communications.

Feedback & Reviews

We may collect customer reviews and testimonials to improve our services. Publicly shared reviews may be displayed on our website unless otherwise requested.

Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a data breach, we will notify affected users and relevant authorities as required by applicable Indian data protection laws.

Data Protection Officer

We have appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO) responsible for overseeing compliance with privacy regulations and addressing data-related concerns.

Accountability & Compliance

Invictus Experiences maintains transparency in data handling practices and cooperates fully with regulatory authorities to ensure compliance.

External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices and encourage reviewing their policies.

Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of India. Any disputes arising shall fall under the jurisdiction of Indian courts.
