Sar Pass Trek: A High-Altitude Himalayan Expedition

Sar Pass Trek is one of the most thrilling high-altitude expeditions in Himachal Pradesh, starting from the scenic village of Kasol in Parvati Valley. Rising to an elevation of approximately 4,200 meters, this trek offers a perfect blend of snow trails, alpine meadows, dense forests, and panoramic Himalayan views.

History and Significance: Sar Pass, meaning “Lake Pass,” is named after a small frozen lake located near the summit. Over the years, it has become one of the most popular beginner-to-intermediate high-altitude treks in India. The trail introduces trekkers to diverse Himalayan terrain while maintaining a strong spirit of adventure and endurance.

Trekking Experience: The journey begins from Kasol and moves through charming villages like Grahan, dense pine forests, high meadows such as Min Thach and Nagaru, and finally to the breathtaking Sar Pass summit. Trekkers experience forest ridges, snow slopes, ridge walks, and dramatic alpine descents toward Biskeri Thach.

Highlights:
Scenic trails through Parvati Valley forests.
High-altitude camping at Nagaru and Biskeri Thach.
Snow-covered ascent to Sar Pass (4,200 m).
Panoramic views of the Pir Panjal ranges.
Adventurous summit push and alpine descent.

Best Time to Visit: The ideal trekking season is from May to October. During early summer, trekkers can experience snow at higher elevations, while later months offer clear skies and lush green meadows.

Note: Sar Pass is a high-altitude trek requiring good fitness and preparation. Carry proper trekking gear, layered clothing, and stay hydrated throughout the expedition. Follow responsible trekking practices to preserve the fragile Himalayan ecosystem.

As we climb higher, the air becomes colder and thinner, and the surroundings turn more dramatic — with snow patches, sweeping valley views, and rising peaks on the horizon. This is where the trek becomes truly alpine.

By early afternoon, we reach Nagaru Campsite (~3,800–4,000 m), one of the highest and most stunning camp zones of the trek. Known for its breathtaking sunsets and clear night skies, Nagaru offers a surreal Himalayan experience.

Day 07: Nagaru to Biskeri Thach (via Sar Pass)

Route: Nagaru → Sar Pass Summit → Long Descent → Biskeri Thach

Summit Day – Crossing the Majestic Sar Pass

Today is the most awaited and rewarding day of your trek! We begin early from Nagaru High Camp after a warm morning drink. With headlamps on and layered clothing, the team starts the ascent toward the Sar Pass Summit.

The climb is steep but incredibly beautiful — snow-covered ridges, Himalayan silence, and golden sunrise views as we approach the top. …Know More

The trail from Nagaru to Sar Pass is approximately 6–7 km and takes 4–6 hours depending on weather and group pace. You will walk entirely over snow terrain, guided carefully by our trek leaders.

Reaching the Sar Pass Summit (~4,200 m) is an unforgettable moment — breathtaking 360° views of towering Himalayan ranges, glacier-carved valleys, and endless white landscapes. We take time for celebration, group photos, and a well-deserved break.

After enjoying the summit, the descent toward Biskeri Thach begins. This section includes thrilling snow slides, a long descending trail, and shifts from snowy terrain to lush green meadows as altitude decreases.

The total descent distance is 8–10 km (approx. 4–5 hours) and brings us to the stunning campsite of Biskeri Thach by afternoon.

Biskeri Thach is known for its vast grasslands, surrounding pine forests, and majestic valley views — a well-earned reward after summit day.

Enjoy warm snacks, relax your legs, and capture the beautiful scenery. After dinner, rest early — your body needs recovery after this long, iconic crossing.

Health & Environment Tip: Today involves both high-altitude ascent and long descent. Walk slowly, hydrate regularly, and protect yourself from sun + snow glare with sunglasses. Respect snow zones and avoid littering.
